# Photo credits

Every photo in the screenshots and animations in this folder comes from
[Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org) and is released into the public
domain under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The
license on each file page was checked when the photos were downloaded
(October 2026). No photos from anyone's private library are used.

For the demo the photos were downloaded as Commons thumbnails, scaled to at
most 2400 px on the long side and saved as JPEG with their original metadata
copied across. The changes beyond that are listed under each set.
